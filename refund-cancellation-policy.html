Refund & Cancellation Policy

How cancellations and refunds work.

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how subscriptions to the BIS Connect platform, provided by BIS CONNECT (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED ("BIS Connect", "we", "us" or "our"), may be cancelled and when refunds may apply. It should be read together with our Terms & Conditions and Payment Policy.

On this page

  • Cancelling a subscription
  • Refund eligibility
  • Non-refundable circumstances
  • Duplicate or incorrect payments
  • How to request a refund
  • Review & processing timeline
  • Contact us

1. Cancelling a subscription

You may cancel your subscription at any time by contacting us using the details below. When you cancel, your subscription will remain active until the end of the current paid billing period, after which it will not renew and access to the paid Services will end. Cancellation stops future charges; it does not, by itself, refund amounts already paid, except where a refund is due under this policy.

2. Refund eligibility

We want you to be satisfied with the Services. Refunds may be considered in cases such as:

  • A verified duplicate charge or a payment taken in error.
  • A charge for a subscription period during which the paid Services were not made available due to a fault on our side that we were unable to resolve.
  • Any circumstance where a refund is required under applicable law.

Eligible refund requests should be submitted within 7 days of the relevant charge.

Where a refund is approved, it is issued to the original payment method used for the transaction, through the same payment channel where possible.

3. Non-refundable circumstances

Refunds are generally not available for:

  • Subscription time that has already been used or made available to you.
  • Cancellations made partway through a paid billing period (access continues to the end of that period instead).
  • Charges arising from your failure to cancel before a renewal, where the Services were available as agreed.
  • Third-party fees or charges that are outside our control.

4. Duplicate or incorrect payments

If you believe you have been charged twice for the same subscription, or charged an incorrect amount, contact us with the payment details and reference. Once we verify the error with our records and the payment provider, we will correct it and refund any amount that was overpaid.

5. How to request a refund

To request a refund, email us at bisconnect.info@gmail.com from the contact associated with your account and include:

  • Your account or business name.
  • The payment date, amount and any payment reference or receipt.
  • A brief description of the reason for the request.

6. Review & processing timeline

We aim to review eligible refund requests within 5 to 7 business days after receiving the information required to verify the transaction. Where a refund is approved, settlement by your bank or payment provider may take additional time that is outside BIS Connect's control. We will keep you informed throughout.
