FAQ

Common questions

What is BIS Connect?

BIS Connect is a cloud based ISP management platform. It brings customer management, MikroTik operations, packages, billing, revenue and support into one system, available on web and mobile.

Who is BIS Connect for?

It is built for internet service providers in Pakistan, from smaller operators to growing networks, and for the teams who run their day to day operations.

Does BIS Connect work with MikroTik?

Yes. BIS Connect integrates with MikroTik RouterOS to provision customers, suspend and reactivate service, and keep status in sync with billing across one or many routers.

Can ISP admins use BIS Connect from mobile?

Yes. Administrators and staff can run core operations from the mobile app, including the dashboard, customers, router status, billing and support.

Is there a customer app?

Yes. Subscribers can view their current bill, invoice history and package, request upgrades, raise complaints and manage their profile.

Does BIS Connect create invoices?

Yes. Invoices are generated on a recurring schedule as PDF documents, with paid, pending and overdue status tracked for each customer.

Can customers submit complaints?

Yes. Customers can raise complaints by category with a description and attachments, and both customers and admins can track them to resolution.

Does BIS Connect support multiple roles?

Yes. Access is role based, so administrators, staff and customers each see only what their role allows, with tenant isolation between ISPs.

How are payments handled?

BIS Connect records cash payments and is built for integrated billing. Digital payment gateway integrations, including PayFast, are being added and are not yet live. A sub-admin (reseller) tier is planned and marked coming soon.
