About BIS Connect

A Pakistani technology company building practical business software

BIS Connect is operated by BIS CONNECT (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED, an SECP-incorporated software and IT company in Pakistan, with its registered office in Punjab.

We design, build and maintain software that solves everyday operational problems for service businesses. Our flagship platform, BIS Connect, helps internet service providers and other subscription-based businesses manage customers, billing, payments, networks and support from a single, reliable system.

Alongside our own platform, we provide software development, IT consultancy, systems analysis and design, quality assurance and telecom-related software services to our clients.

Our mission

To give service businesses affordable, dependable software that automates the repetitive work of running operations, so teams can focus on their customers.

Our vision

To become a trusted technology partner for ISPs and growing businesses across Pakistan and beyond, delivering software engineered to a high professional standard.

Engineered for real operations

Built from real-world ISP requirements — multi-tenant, cloud-hosted, and designed for teams that need automation they can trust every day.

The BIS Connect Platform

Everything an ISP needs to run, in one place

BIS Connect is a multi-tenant, cloud-based platform with dedicated portals for the platform operator, service-provider administrators and their customers.

Customer & account management

Onboard and manage customers, subscriptions and account status across your whole base.

Packages & plans

Define service packages and assign them to customers with the terms that fit your business.

Automated billing & invoices

Bills generated on schedule with downloadable PDF invoices — no manual monthly workload.

Payment tracking

Record cash payments and accept online payments through approved third-party gateways, with clear payment history.

Network automation (MikroTik)

Activate or suspend service and view live sessions across one or many routers — kept in sync with billing.

Staff, admin & reseller roles

Role-based access for administrators, staff and downstream resellers with data isolation between tenants.

Complaints & support

Built-in support conversations so customer issues are logged, tracked and resolved in one thread.

Notifications & reminders

Push notifications, SMS billing reminders and broadcast messages to keep customers informed.

Dashboards & reporting

Revenue, key metrics, system health and an audit log give administrators a clear operational picture.

Subscription-based access

Delivered as a subscription SaaS — customers access the service online with no hardware to buy.
