Payment Policy

How payments are accepted and processed.

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This Payment Policy explains how payments for the BIS Connect platform and related services, provided by BIS CONNECT (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED ("BIS Connect", "we", "us" or "our"), are accepted and processed. It should be read together with our Terms & Conditions and Refund & Cancellation Policy.

On this page

  • Accepted payment methods
  • Third-party payment gateways
  • Payment security
  • Payment confirmation
  • Failed or pending payments
  • Currency & charges
  • Contact us

1. Accepted payment methods

Payments for BIS Connect services may be made through the methods we make available to you, which may include online payment through approved third-party payment gateways and, where arranged, other agreed methods. The available methods may change over time, and only verified, approved methods are offered.

2. Third-party payment gateways

Online payments may be processed securely through approved third-party payment gateways. We are integrating PayFast as a payment gateway to process online payments for our services. When you pay online, you may be directed to the gateway's secure environment to complete your payment.

BIS Connect does not publicly expose any payment-gateway credentials, merchant identifiers, secured keys or API keys. These are confidential and are never displayed on this website.

3. Payment security

Card and banking details entered during online payment are handled by the payment gateway under their own security standards. BIS Connect does not store your full card or banking credentials. We may keep limited transaction records, such as a payment reference, amount, date and status, to confirm payment and maintain accurate billing history.

4. Payment confirmation

When a payment is completed successfully, it is recorded against your account and reflected in your billing history. Where applicable, a confirmation is sent to the email associated with your account, and the relevant services are updated in line with the payment.

5. Failed or pending payments

If a payment fails or remains pending, the related service or invoice will stay unpaid until the payment is successfully completed, and you may be prompted to try again or use another method. If an amount is debited but not confirmed to us, contact us with the transaction details so we can verify it with the payment provider and reconcile your account. Duplicate or incorrect charges are handled under our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

6. Currency & charges

Unless stated otherwise, payments are processed in Pakistani Rupees (PKR). Any bank or payment-provider charges that may apply to a transaction are outside our control.
