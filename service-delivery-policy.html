Service Delivery Policy

How we deliver access to our digital services.

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This Service Delivery Policy describes how BIS CONNECT (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED ("BIS Connect", "we", "us" or "our") delivers its services to customers.

On this page

  • Digital services only
  • Activation process
  • Activation timing
  • How you receive access
  • Support
  • Contact us

1. Digital services only

BIS Connect provides digital software services. Access to the BIS Connect platform is delivered online as a subscription. There is no physical product and nothing is shipped for BIS Connect subscriptions. Any professional services (such as consultancy or custom development) are delivered digitally and/or remotely as agreed with you.

2. Activation process

After you sign up and your subscription is confirmed, we set up your account and enable access to the Services. Depending on your requirements, onboarding may include creating your administrator account, configuring your service packages, and, where applicable, assisting with connecting your network.

3. Activation timing

Straightforward accounts are typically activated within one business day after subscription/payment confirmation and receipt of required setup information. For configurations that require onboarding or technical setup, additional time may be required, and we will keep you informed of the expected timing.

4. How you receive access

Once your account is active, you receive access through the BIS Connect platform using your account credentials. Confirmation and important account information are sent to the email address associated with your account. It is your responsibility to keep your contact details accurate so that you receive these communications.

5. Support

Our team is available to help with onboarding, configuration and everyday questions. You can reach support using the contact details below, and issues can also be raised through the platform's built-in support features where available. We aim to respond to support requests in a timely manner during our normal working hours.
