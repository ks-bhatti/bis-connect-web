Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BIS CONNECT (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED ("BIS Connect", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, discloses and safeguards information when you use our website and the BIS Connect platform and related services (together, the "Services"). By using the Services, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

We collect information needed to provide the Services:

  • Account information, such as name, business name, email address, phone number and role, provided when an account is created.
  • Customer & billing records, where a service provider uses BIS Connect to manage their own customers, this may include customer contact details, subscription packages, invoices and payment status entered into the platform.
  • Payment information, see the dedicated section below.
  • Technical information, such as device, browser and log data generated when you access the Services, used for security and reliability.
  • Communications, messages, support requests and enquiries you send to us.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, operate, maintain and improve the Services.
  • To generate bills and invoices and to record and track payments.
  • To send service-related notifications, reminders and important account messages.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your enquiries.
  • To protect the security and integrity of the Services and prevent misuse or fraud.
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Payment information

Where online payments are offered, they are processed by approved third-party payment gateways. Payment card and banking details are entered with, and handled by, the payment gateway.

We do not store your full card or banking credentials. BIS Connect may retain limited transaction records, such as a payment reference, amount, date and status, to confirm payment and maintain accurate billing history, but sensitive payment credentials are handled by the payment provider under their own security standards.

4. Cookies & analytics

Our website uses only the cookies and similar technologies necessary for the site to function and to understand basic, aggregated usage. We do not use them to build advertising profiles. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling some cookies may affect how parts of the site work.

5. Third-party services

We share information only as needed to run the Services, for example with:

  • Payment gateways, to process transactions you initiate.
  • Infrastructure, hosting and communication providers that help us operate the platform.
  • Authorities or advisors where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Information security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect information, including access controls, role-based permissions and tenant data isolation within the platform. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and to respond promptly to any issues.

7. Data retention

We retain information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Services, and thereafter as required to meet legal, accounting, tax or reporting obligations, or to resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise it.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to access, correct or delete your personal information, or ask questions about how it is used. Where BIS Connect processes customer records on behalf of a service provider, requests relating to those records should be directed to the service provider who controls that data. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

9. Children's privacy

The Services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
